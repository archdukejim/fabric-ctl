| Purpose | fabric-agent's check of a call, console and CLI alike (manual 1.6.12.2, 1.6.12.11; 2.1.6.66 to 2.1.6.68, 2.1.6.73), made inside the DC from the directory as it is (grants_tool's authorize): the tiers (nothing below a tier touches it or its members), the grants' people and machines rights, a group's two levels (its scope and managers by the tier above, its members by that tier or its managers within their controls).
