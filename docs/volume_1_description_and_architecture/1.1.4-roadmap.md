## 1.1.4.1 How to read it

This is the one ordered list of fabric's work (owner request 2026-10-04, after the alignment to the global rules,
2.3.1.3). The phases (1.1.3), the federation milestones (1.9.7), the Samba build plan (1.6.4.12) and the deferred
work (2.3.1.7) say *what* each piece is; **this chapter alone says in which order they are built**. A change of
order is an owner decision, recorded here with its date. Each item keeps the name it has in its own chapter.

## 1.1.4.5 Names that look alike

- **F1–F4 phases** (1.9.7.1: trust and names, identity, HA, relays) are not **decisions F1–F10** (named so before the re-baseline; now 2.1.9.1–2.1.9.8, 2.1.5.1 and 2.1.5.2).
  "F3" in 2.3.1.7 and 1.6.4 means **decision F3**, sharing between sites; the HA work is **phase F3**.
- **S1–S6 decisions** (named so before the re-baseline; now 2.1.6.6–2.1.6.10 and 2.1.5.3) are not the **S0–S8 build steps** (1.6.4.12).
- **M-milestones** (1.9.7) number the federation build; **phases** (1.1.3) number the product's first build.
