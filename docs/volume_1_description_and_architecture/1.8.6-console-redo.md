## 1.8.6.1 Status and scope

**Designed 2026-10-17 for 0.8.0; being built** (decisions 2.1.8.8 to 2.1.8.16): what is built so far is in
1.8.6.11. 0.8.0 is the console's release
(2.1.1.58): the frame of [1.8.3.2](1.8.3-single-pane.md#1832-the-consoles-frame-a-navigation-down-the-left-as-cockpit-has-it)
is built, and every page is restyled to one pattern, with one way to save. The domain pages are designed in
[1.6.12](1.6.12-domain-management.md). Kept: the security model (1.8.2: mTLS where asked, Keycloak sign-in, CSRF, the
strict Content-Security-Policy with no inline script or style), the privilege split (the console reaches the host only
through fabric-agent, 1.3.3), the permissions and bundles (1.6.8.1), and every route (old addresses keep working: a page
that moves answers its old address with a redirect).

## 1.8.6.2 The frame

As 1.8.3.2, with what 0.8.0 adds:

- **The top bar:** the install's name and host, the version, the pending changes of the page shown (1.8.6.5), and the
  person signed in (their menu: My account, sign out).
- **The navigation down the left:** one entry per part, its pages below it; the entry of the page shown is open. A
  part not installed shows its page with how to turn it on. An entry appears for anyone who may see one of its pages.
- **Narrow screens** (under 768 px): the navigation folds into a button in the top bar (CSS only, no script).
- **Nothing in the frame needs script**: the page works with JavaScript off; script only improves (live job progress).

## 1.8.6.3 The page pattern

Every page is built from the same few parts, shared as Jinja macros in `templates/webui-app/`:

- **A list** of what the page manages (a table on a wide screen; cards under 768 px, so nothing scrolls sideways). A
  long list has search and paging; a row opens its **detail page**.
- **A detail page** for one thing (a person, a machine, a zone): its facts in cards, its related things linked (a
  person's devices, a machine's 802.1X device), and its actions.
- **The actions bar** along the top of a list or detail page: the page's actions as buttons, named with a verb and
  what they act on ("Add person", "Reset password", "Move to OU"). A person without the permission does not see the
  button (and never sees an empty card).
- **Forms** open on their own page or in a section of the detail page, never inside a table cell.
- **Status** is a coloured light with a text label (`role="img"`, `aria-label`), never colour alone.
- **Empty states** say what would be here and the action that adds the first one.
- **Copy:** short, plain, no manual numbers in what people read (those go in a "Learn more" link to Volume 4).
- **Messages** after an action come from the server's own result, never from the address bar (the query-string
  `msg`/`err` flash goes: 1.8.6.7).

## 1.8.6.5 Apply per page

Decision 2.1.8.9. Every change a page makes is held as **pending** until that page's Apply:

- **What is a page** for Apply: a navigation page (DNS → Zones, Directory → People, ...). Its detail pages share its
  pending changes (a person's reset is pending on People).
- **What is held:** each change as the agent call it will make, with what it changes (old and new value) and the
  version of the thing it changes. Held by fabric-agent, not the browser, so a reload or another tab sees them.
- **The bar:** while a page has pending changes, the top bar shows "N changes pending" with **Review**, **Apply** and
  **Discard**. Review lists each change (and lets one be dropped). The navigation marks every page that has some.
- **Apply** runs the page's changes in order as one job (its progress on its job page, 1.8.4.2); it stops at the first
  that fails and says which, leaving the rest pending. A change whose thing changed since it was made (another person
  applied first) is refused and shown again, as 1.8.5.5 does.
- **One page's Apply never applies another's.** The whole-install apply (today's Overview and Directory → Domain
  "Apply changes") is replaced: a page whose changes need the services re-rendered (the password policy, DNS settings)
  re-renders and restarts only what its changes touch, health-gated (2.1.14.15). Its permission is the page's own
  write permission (no more `dns:write` for an apply of anything, 1.8.6.7).
- **Done at once, never pending:** only a person changing their own password (1.8.6.6). An admin's actions on one
  person or machine (a reset, a disable, a move) are pending like any other change (owner, 2026-10-17).
- **Audit:** each applied change is audited as it is today, with who made it and who applied it.
- **Held per person** (owner, 2026-10-17): each person sees, reviews and applies only their own pending changes. Another
  person's are not shown as pending, but their effect once applied is (below).
- **The queue, shown as a stream:** an applied change that still waits on something (BIND's `rndc` reload, a service's
  restart and health check, AD's replication to another DC) is shown live, change by change, as it moves through:
  queued, running, done or failed with why. The stream is the job page's (1.8.4.2), reached from the top bar while
  anything of the page is in the queue; every person who may see the page sees its queue, whoever applied it.

## 1.8.6.6 My account

Decision 2.1.8.11. Every person of the domain may sign in to the console; one with no fabric role sees only this:

- **Change my password:** the current one and the new one twice, checked against the domain's policy; written to AD
  at once by fabric-agent, as the person (2.1.1.62; no longer Keycloak's account page), sessions elsewhere ended.
- **Sign-in factors:** TOTP and passkeys, added and removed (Keycloak's own pages, linked, until 1.0 or later:
  2.1.1.62).
- **My certificates** (issued to them by fabric) and **my devices** (802.1X devices they own), read-only.
- **Groups I manage** (2.1.6.67): each group they are a manager of, its members, and adding or removing members as its
  controls allow; nothing else about the group.
- **Forgotten password:** not reset from the sign-in page (fabric sends no mail); the sign-in page says to ask the
  helpdesk.

The console's sign-in therefore no longer requires `fabric-console-admin` (`webui_admin_role`, 1.6.10): any person of
the realm signs in, and each page still checks its own permission. A person whose account is disabled cannot sign in.

## 1.8.6.10 Settled points (owner, 2026-10-17)

1. **Pending changes are each person's own**; once applied, the queue is shown to all as a stream (1.8.6.5).
2. **Settings are the application's; policies live where they apply.** The Settings entry holds the application's own
   settings: the setup wizard and its file (1.8.5), and what Security holds today (second factors, the console's
   client certificate). Security as an entry goes. The domain's password policy and Kerberos sign-in move to
   Directory (1.6.12.9, 1.6.12.10), and the wizard's sign-in step keeps only the application's sign-in settings, with a
   link to Directory → Policies. The audit log moves to the Overview entry as a page.
3. **The password policy in the console covers everyone but the top-level administrators.** Theirs is set on the host
   only (`fabricctl`, 1.6.12.10). Revised 2026-10-18 by 2.1.6.71: the Directory Administrators' policy is set by
   `fabric-admin`, `fabric-admin`'s own on the host only.

## 1.8.6.11 As built

### 1.8.6.11.1 Step 1: the frame, the navigation and the moved addresses (2026-10-22)

- **The navigation** is one table, `NAV` in `src/webui/views/constants.py`: each entry, its pages and the permissions
  that show each page (any one of them). `nav_menu` keeps an entry when one of its pages may be seen and links it to
  the first such page; the open entry lists its pages, the page shown marked `aria-current="page"`.
- **The pages' addresses:** Overview `/` (Health), `/overview/doctor`, `/overview/updates`, `/audit`; DNS
  `/dns/zones`, `/dns-filter`; DHCP `/kea`; PKI `/pki`, `/pki/acme`; Directory `/directory/people`,
  `/directory/domain`, `/directory/machines`, `/directory/gpo` (the pages of 1.6.12 come in step 5); 802.1X `/8021x`
  (RADIUS clients), `/8021x/devices[/<name>]`, `/8021x/roles[/<name>]`, `/8021x/decisions`, `/8021x/guides`; Secrets
  `/secrets`, `/secrets/unlock`, `/secrets/disk` (and the unlock methods' own forms under `/secrets/<view>`);
  Federation `/federation`; Settings `/settings` (the wizard), `/settings/sign-in` (sign-in security). The forms keep
  their addresses (`/bind9/...`, `/stepca/...`, `/openbao/...`, `/freeradius/...`, `/directory/...`).
- **Moved addresses:** `src/webui/routes/moved_to.py` answers each old address of 1.8.6.4 with a 303 to its new one,
  keeping the query's page keys and dropping `msg` and `err`.
- **Messages:** a form's outcome is kept in the person's session and shown once, on the next page
  (`Handler.back`); a page never reads one from the address. Back from a step-up sign-in, `?signed_in=1` shows a
  fixed text of the server's own.
- **A page for one thing that is not there** answers 404 (2.1.8.16). The DNS filter's page is refused (403) without
  `dns:filter` or `dns:querylog`, as its navigation entry is.
- **The top bar** shows the host and domain (fabric-agent's version route carries them), the version and the person's
  menu; the pending changes join it in step 3.
- **The dev preview** runs the production routes and pages over a fake agent in the same process
  (`src/webui/devpreview/`), which checks the real permission table (`required_permission`), so what the preview shows
  a bundle is what production shows it.
- Tests and what was checked: [2.3.8.3.1](../volume_2_engineering_decisions/2.3.8-webui-notes.md#2383-the-console-redo-080-as-built-and-tested).

### 1.8.6.11.3 Step 3: Apply per page (2026-10-22)

- **The store** is fabric-agent's `PendingStore` (`src/agent/pending_store.py`), in memory (2.1.8.13): each person's
  changes, each held on the page fabric-agent names for its route (`pending_thing.pending_page_of`: People, Machines
  with its host groups and sudo rules, Domain (the password policy), Group Policy; Groups, OUs, Access and Kerberos
  ready for step 5). A change is its agent route and body, unchanged (2.3.6.20.2), with what it changes in a sentence,
  the old values (the thing's fields the body names, as read now, or for an enable, disable or removal what it
  flips: `enabled`, `exists`) and the new, and its version: the SHA-256 of the
  agent's own read of the thing (2.1.8.14). Holding checks the change's own permission; so does Apply, change by
  change, with the person's permissions then. At most 200 held per person.
- **Apply** runs the page's changes in order as one job. Each is checked for its permission, then its thing is read
  again: a different version means someone changed it since, so the change is held again against the thing as it is
  now (marked so on the Review) and the job stops there. A thing an earlier change of the same apply changed is not
  checked again. The first failure stops the job and says why; the changes after it are not run and stay pending.
  The Domain page's Apply ends with its settings written into the services (fabric's apply, restarting only what
  changed, health-gated: 2.1.14.15), so no whole-install Apply is needed there.
- **The queue:** each page keeps its last 10 applies for 30 minutes, change by change (queued, running, done, failed
  with why, not run), shown to everyone with the page's own read permission. A result that is a one-time password
  (a new person's, a reset's, a new machine's) is never in the queue or the job: it is shown once, to who applied
  it, on the page's Review, then forgotten.
- **Routes:** fabric-agent `GET /v1/pending`, `/v1/pending/<page>`, `POST /v1/pending/hold`,
  `/v1/pending/<page>/{drop,discard,apply}` (1.3.3). The console: the top bar's "N changes pending" with Review,
  Apply and Discard on the page shown, "Applying…" while one runs; the navigation's count per page; the Review at
  `/changes/<page>` (refreshing itself while an apply runs: a meta refresh, no script); the forms
  `/changes/<page>/{apply,discard,drop}`.
- **The Directory pages' forms** (People: add, reset, disable, enable, remove, groups; Machines: add, enable, disable,
  remove, host groups, sudo rules; Domain: the password policy; Group Policy: set, clear) hold their change instead
  of making it. Devices and device roles (802.1X) and the other entries keep acting at once until step 5 rebuilds
  them in the pattern.
- Tests and what was checked: [2.3.8.3.3](../volume_2_engineering_decisions/2.3.8-webui-notes.md#2383-the-console-redo-080-as-built-and-tested).

### 1.8.6.11.4 Step 4: My account and sign-in without a role (2026-10-23)

- **Sign-in:** a person whose token carries no fabric permission now signs in (audited `LOGIN`, "no fabric role: My
  account only") and sees only the My account entry; `/` sends anyone who may not see the Overview to the first page
  they may see. The client certificate, when required, and Keycloak's own checks (a disabled account cannot sign in)
  are unchanged.
- **My account** (`/account`), every signed-in person's: their details as the DC has them, read-only (the 15 fields of
  2.1.6.93, the groups they are in; the helpdesk changes them); **Change my password** (current, new, new again;
  done at once through fabric-agent, never pending, 1.6.12.17.9; the two new ones compared by the console first, no
  password ever in an address or a message); **Sign-in factors** (a link to Keycloak's own page of their account);
  **My certificates** (fabric's ledger, the subject's `CN` their user name, with each one's status) and **My
  devices** (the 802.1X devices they own). fabric-agent `GET /v1/account` (1.3.3), always the token's user.
- **Groups I manage** (`/account/groups`): each group they manage with its members, and adding or removing members as
  its controls allow. Each change waits on My account's own Apply (the pending page `account`, the Review at
  `/changes/account`), held as `/v1/account/groups/<name>/members` and run as the group's members route, so the DC's
  check of the managers decides at Apply as it does at once elsewhere. My account's queue shows each person only their
  own applies.
- **Each page's permission, in the console too:** a navigation page whose permission the person lacks answers 403
  before anything is read (and a page's Review with it).
- Tests and what was checked: [2.3.8.3.5](../volume_2_engineering_decisions/2.3.8-webui-notes.md#23835-step-4-my-account-2026-10-23).

### 1.8.6.11.5 Step 5: the Directory's Overview and People (2026-10-23)

- **Overview** (`/directory`, `domain:read`; first of the Directory's pages): the domain and its NetBIOS name, its
  functional level, its DCs (writable or read-only), the five roles named plainly, this DC's replication (each link
  on Federation), the sites (from the `site-<name>` groups) and the counts of people, groups, computers and OUs, each
  linking to its page once that page exists. fabric-agent `GET /v1/directory` (1.3.3); a directory that cannot be read
  is said on the page, the domain's facts still shown.
- **People** (`/directory/people`): the list (state light, name linking to the person's page, user name, e-mail,
  state, last sign-in: AD's `lastLogonTimestamp`, up to 14 days behind), searched by any column (`q`, every word
  matched) and paged 50 a page (`page`); **Add person** on its own page (`/directory/people/_new`, `people:create`).
  No form sits in a row any more: every action is on the person's page.
- **A person's page** (`/directory/people/<uid>`, `people:read`; fabric-agent `GET /v1/people/<uid>`): state and last
  sign-in, sites, the 15 fields of 2.1.6.93, groups, the certificates fabric issued them (the ledger, by the
  subject's `CN`) and their 802.1X devices (linked to 802.1X). The actions bar: **Reset password**, **Unlock** (only
  when locked out), **Disable** or **Enable**; then **Edit details** (`people:edit`; the console reads the person
  afresh and sends only the fields that differ, so a form changes nothing it did not change; an edit that changes
  nothing is refused before it is held), **Groups** (take out of each group, add to one of the site's), and
  **Remove** with the user name typed back. Each waits for People's Apply and comes back to the person's page (a
  removal to People). The console checks People's permission for the person's page and Add person too (403).
- **Removed:** the People page's `_form` routes (the person picked from a list in a form): every change names the
  person in its address now. `/directory` no longer redirects to People.
- Tests and what was checked: [2.3.8.3.6](../volume_2_engineering_decisions/2.3.8-webui-notes.md#23836-step-5a-the-directorys-overview-and-people-2026-10-23).

### 1.8.6.11.6 Step 5: the Directory's Groups (2026-10-23)

- **Groups** (`/directory/groups`, `domain:read`): the site's groups with their kind (the site's, a role bundle's,

- Each change waits for the Groups page's Apply (the pending page `groups`); fabric-agent decides who may make it, at
  hold and at Apply (1.6.12.17.5).
- **The list's search and paging** moved to one helper (`views/paged.py`) and one pager macro (`pattern.html`), for
  People and Groups; each list's search form stays written out in its template, so the entry-point inventory reads its

- Tests and what was checked: [2.3.8.3.7](../volume_2_engineering_decisions/2.3.8-webui-notes.md#23837-step-5b-the-directorys-groups-2026-10-23).
