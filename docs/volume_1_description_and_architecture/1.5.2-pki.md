# 1.5.2 PKI and certificates

Topics:
- 1.5.2.1 PKI & Certificates (Step-CA)

## 1.5.2.1 PKI & Certificates (Step-CA)
These variables define how the internal Certificate Authority generates and signs certificates.

### 1.5.2.1.1 `ca_name`
**Description:** The name of the CA: passed to `step ca init --name` at the first Step-CA initialisation (Step-CA derives the root and intermediate CNs from it), and shown on the web pages.

**Default Value:** `friendly_name` + `" CA"`

**Immutable:** Yes 🔒

**Effected Jinja Templates:**
- `nginx/www/certs/index.html.j2`, `nginx/www/certs/install-{chrome,firefox,python}-ubuntu.sh.j2`
- `nginx/www/landing/index.html.j2`, `nginx/www/manual/index.html.j2`, `nginx/www/shared/base.html.j2`
- `vars.yaml.j2`
- read by `fabriclib/setup/init_pki.py`

The subject fields `cert_country`, `cert_province`, `cert_city`, `cert_org` and `cert_ou` go into the Step-CA certificate templates (`leaf.tpl`, `subca.tpl`), which are used for ACME certificates, CSRs signed from the web UI, and key pairs or sub-CAs minted by fabric. Setup's own service certificates are issued without a template. The templates are re-rendered on every apply, so a change affects new certificates only.

### 1.5.2.1.7 `cert_root_ca_days`
**Description:** The root CA's lifetime in days (decision 2.1.5.6), asked in setup's Advanced in years before the CA exists; the intermediate lives a year less. At least 365. Fixed once the CA exists: on an existing install a value different from the root's actual lifetime is refused with the reason (a brought-in root's is not checked). Applied from 0.6.3; before, the root was always ten years and this setting was not read.

**Default Value:** `3650` (10 years)

**Immutable:** Yes 🔒

**Effected Jinja Templates:**
- `vars.yaml.j2`

### 1.5.2.1.8 `cert_root_digest`
**Description:** Intended as the signature hash algorithm for the Root CA. **Not applied:** `step ca init` runs with Step-CA's own defaults; fabric does not pass this setting, and nothing else reads it.

**Default Value:** `"sha512"`

**Immutable:** Yes 🔒

**Effected Jinja Templates:**

### 1.5.2.1.15 `cert_service_days`
**Description:** The validity (in days) of the service certificates fabric mints for nginx, BIND9, the domain controller and the other services (decision 2.1.5.4), and the default of `stepca_cert_max_lifetime_hours`. A service certificate living longer than this is re-issued (2.1.5.7: how the upgrade to 0.6.3 replaces the 365-day ones). Must leave at least 7 days after `cert_renew_after_days`.

**Default Value:** `47` (from 0.6.3; 365 before)

**Effected Jinja Templates:**
- `vars.yaml.j2` (`stepca_cert_max_lifetime_hours`)
- read by `fabriclib/pki/mint_cert.py`

### 1.5.2.1.16 `pki_manual_max_days`
**Description:** The longest validity (in days) of a certificate issued by hand from the web UI's Step-CA tab — a signed CSR or a generated key pair. Requests above it are refused.

**Default Value:** `1825` (5 years)

**Effected Jinja Templates:**
- `vars.yaml.j2`
- read by `fabriclib/pki/common/valid_days.py`, `fabriclib/pki/ca_summary.py`

### 1.5.2.1.17 `cert_acme_lifetime_hours`
**Description:** The default and maximum validity of certificates requested via ACME (the `acme` provisioner in Step-CA's `ca.json`). Written only at the first Step-CA initialisation.

**Default Value:** `"1128h"` (47 days, from 0.6.3; `"720h"` before)

**Immutable:** Yes 🔒

**Effected Jinja Templates:**
- `vars.yaml.j2`
- read by `fabriclib/setup/init_pki.py`

### 1.5.2.1.18 `stepca_port`

**Default Value:** `9000`

### 1.5.2.1.19 `stepca_cert_allow_subordinate_ca`
**Description:** Whether Step-CA's JWK provisioner may issue the basicConstraints extension, i.e. sign subordinate CA certificates. Written only at the first Step-CA initialisation.

**Default Value:** `true`

**Immutable:** Yes 🔒

**Effected Jinja Templates:**

### 1.5.2.1.20 `stepca_cert_max_lifetime_hours`
**Description:** The longest lifetime Step-CA will issue a certificate for (`maxTLSCertDuration` for the whole authority). Written only at the first Step-CA initialisation.

**Default Value:** `cert_service_days` × 24, in hours (`"1128h"`)

**Immutable:** Yes 🔒

**Effected Jinja Templates:**
- `vars.yaml.j2`
- read by `fabriclib/setup/init_pki.py`


### 1.5.2.1.21 Bring Your Own Certificates (BYOC)
If you already possess a securely offline-generated Root and Intermediate CA, you can import them instead of letting Step-CA mint its own.

### 1.5.2.1.22 `byoc`
**Description:** Set to `true` to import your own root and intermediate at the first Step-CA initialisation (setup stops if any of the three files is missing). The intermediate key may be unencrypted or encrypted with fabric's `ca_password` (a federation site's is: [federation.md](1.9.1-sites.md#1911-status)); Step-CA always gets its password file. The root key `step ca init` generated is removed, since it does not belong to your root.

**Default Value:** `false`

**Immutable:** Yes 🔒

**Effected Jinja Templates:**
- `vars.yaml.j2`
- read by `fabriclib/setup/init_pki.py`

### 1.5.2.1.23 `ca_crt_path`
**Description:** Absolute path to your existing Root CA certificate.

**Default Value:** `"/home/default_admin/output/root_ca.crt"`

**Immutable:** Yes 🔒

**Effected Jinja Templates:**
- `vars.yaml.j2`

### 1.5.2.1.24 `ica_crt_path`
**Description:** Absolute path to your existing Intermediate CA certificate.

**Default Value:** `"/home/default_admin/output/ica.crt"`

**Immutable:** Yes 🔒

**Effected Jinja Templates:**
- `vars.yaml.j2`

### 1.5.2.1.25 `ica_key_path`
**Description:** Absolute path to your existing Intermediate CA private key. Only written to `vars.yaml` when set.

**Default Value:** *(None)* — then `ica_crt_path` with its extension replaced by `.key`

**Immutable:** Yes 🔒

### 1.5.2.1.26 `ca_nest_depth`
**Description:** How many levels of federation sites may nest below a site (design [1.9.5.1](1.9.5-attachment.md#1951-attachment-flat-through-a-node-or-nested)). Used once, when this install makes its own root CA: the root gets path length `ca_nest_depth + 1` (0 allows flat sites only, 1 lets a site invited with `--nest 1` hold sites of its own). 0..4. A brought-in root (`byoc`) has its own path length, which setup reports. Fixed once the root exists.

**Default Value:** `1`

**Immutable:** Yes 🔒

**Effected Jinja Templates:**
- `vars.yaml.j2`; read by `fabriclib/setup/init_pki.py`

### 1.5.2.1.27 `ica_parents_path` / `site_ca_depth`
**Description:** Set by `fabricctl setup --join` (and `federation reparent`), not by hand: a nested site's parent CAs (the CA certificates between its intermediate and the root, copied to `stepca/data/certs/ca_parents.crt`) and how many there are. 0 for the root site and flat sites. Every certificate chain this site hands out carries the parents, and nginx's client-certificate depth for the web UI grows by `site_ca_depth`.

**Default Value:** unset / `0`

**Effected Jinja Templates:**
- `nginx/nginx.conf.j2` (`ssl_verify_depth`)
- `vars.yaml.j2`; read by `fabriclib/setup/init_pki.py`

### 1.5.2.1.28 `cert_renew_after_days`
**Description:** The age, in days, at which a service certificate is renewed (decision 2.1.5.4): the daily `fabric-certs.timer` (`fabricctl certs --scheduled`), `fabricctl certs` and setup renew one this old. At least 1, and leaving at least 7 days of `cert_service_days`, so failed nights are retried before anything expires. Added in 0.6.3.

**Default Value:** `30` (with 47-day certificates: renewed with 17 days left)

**Effected Jinja Templates:**
- `vars.yaml.j2`
- read by `fabriclib/setup/mint_service_certs.py`, checked by `fabriclib/pki/check_cert_lifetimes.py`

### 1.5.2.1.29 `scep_challenge_minutes`
**Description:** How long the one-time challenge in an Apple enrolment profile may be used, in minutes (decision 2.1.5.12, [1.5.3.4](1.5.3-device-enrolment.md#1534-the-one-time-challenge)): a whole number, 5 to 1440. Used once in any case. Added in 0.9.

**Default Value:** `60`

**Effected Jinja Templates:**
- `vars.yaml.j2`
- read by `fabriclib/pki/scep/create_challenge.py`, checked by `fabriclib/pki/scep/check_scep_settings.py`

### 1.5.2.1.30 `scep_cert_days`
**Description:** How long a certificate from device enrolment lives, SCEP's and the PKCS#12's ([1.5.3](1.5.3-device-enrolment.md)): 1 to `pki_manual_max_days` days. Written into Step-CA's SCEP provisioner by setup's `pki` step; a change affects new certificates only. Added in 0.9.

**Default Value:** `365`

**Effected Jinja Templates:**
- `vars.yaml.j2`
- read by `fabriclib/pki/scep/configure_scep_provisioner.py`, `fabriclib/pki/scep/enrol_p12.py`, checked by `fabriclib/pki/scep/check_scep_settings.py`
