## 2.3.11.3 What 0.9's 802.1X lab needs

Written 2026-10-10 while the owner builds the lab (2.3.11.2), so the VMs and the tests meet. On gaming-23's Hyper-V,
beside the DHCP lab (2.3.1.9.6, the "Fabric DHCP Testing" private switch):

| Machine | Role | Networks |
|---|---|---|
| `g23-vm-ub24-1` | the fabric host: FreeRADIUS on (`install_freeradius`), DHCP for the lab as today | Default Switch (management), the lab switch |
| `vm-debian-switch` | the authenticator: `hostapd` with its `wired` driver on each supplicant-facing port, RADIUS to the fabric host; dynamic VLANs (`dynamic_vlan=1`, a `vlan_file`, `vlan_tagged_interface` towards fabric) so an accepted port lands on the VLAN fabric returns | one interface towards fabric (the lab switch, 802.1Q trunk), one private switch per supplicant (the "ports") |
| a Windows supplicant | a domain machine and its people: PEAP-MSCHAPv2 (machine and user), EAP-TLS with a fabric certificate | its port switch only |
| an Ubuntu supplicant (`ub22` or `ub26`) | `wpa_supplicant` with its `wired` driver: EAP-TLS (a device certificate), EAP-TTLS/PAP (a person) | its port switch only |

Hyper-V notes: a private switch per supplicant is how the authenticator gets one "port" per machine; the authenticator's
port NICs need MAC address spoofing on (it bridges frames from behind them); VLAN tags on the fabric-facing NIC need the
adapter's trunk mode (`Set-VMNetworkAdapterVlan -Trunk -AllowedVlanIdList ... -NativeVlanId 0`). What the tests check,
in `tests/lab/dot1x.sh` (to be written against these machines): each method accepted with its role's VLAN (the port's
traffic then carries that tag towards fabric, and DHCP serves that VLAN's subnet), refused for the cases the
freeradius suite already refuses in containers, a port with no supplicant getting nothing (or MAB by permission), and
an accepted machine sorted into its OS OU (1.6.11.5) still admitted (PEAP's search is the whole OU=machines subtree).
CoA and RadSec stay vendor questions (2.1.11.1, Open).

Multi-site on the same lab: `g23-vm-ub26-1` (26.04 server, `10.20.0.11` on the lab switch) as a second fabric host
joining `g23-vm-ub24-1` as a site (the federation's real-machine run, beside `tests/samba/site_join.py`'s containers).

## 2.3.11.4 What gaming-23's lab proved, and the move to Incus

Built 2026-10-10 with the owner, on gaming-23's Hyper-V, as 2.3.11.3 asks: the authenticator is a Debian 13 VM
(`debian-vswitch` in SSH, `debian-vswtich` in Hyper-V) with hostapd 2.10 installed, a management NIC on Default
Switch, three port NICs on private switches `g23-8021x-p1` to `p3` (MAC spoofing on), and an uplink trunk
(VLANs 10, 20, 30, native untagged) on "Fabric DHCP Testing"; ub24's lab NIC became the same trunk. ub22 (port 2)
and ub20 (port 3) got a third NIC each on their port switch; port 1 waited for a Windows VM that was not kept.

What it proved, with raw frames before any hostapd or RADIUS:
- **Hyper-V passes 802.1X frames.** EAPOL to the PAE group address (`01:80:C2:00:00:03`) crossed a private switch
  both ways: three EAPOL-Starts from ub22 and from ub20 reached only their own port, and three EAP-Request/Identity
  frames from the authenticator reached ub22. Nothing leaked to another port.
- **The trunk keeps untagged traffic.** Hyper-V accepted `-NativeVlanId 0`; ub22 still reached fabric on
  `10.20.0.10` over the lab switch after ub24's NIC became a trunk.

Owner, 2026-10-10: the lab moves to an **Incus cluster** (two hosts, VLANs on a managed switch) before 802.1X is
tested, and 0.9.0 may wait for it; the Hyper-V lab is taken down. For the rebuild:
- **Linux bridges do not forward EAPOL by default** (802.1D reserves the group address). Each port bridge needs
  `group_fwd_mask` bit 3 (`echo 8 > /sys/class/net/<bridge>/bridge/group_fwd_mask`), or the port is a direct
  link; repeat the raw-frame check above first.
- **One bridge per port, authenticator and supplicants on one member,** as here; the trunk to fabric goes over the
  switch's VLANs.
- **To verify early:** whether hostapd's `wired` driver closes a port that has not authenticated and applies
  dynamic VLANs by itself (2.3.11.3 assumes it does), or needs a `hostapd_cli` action script driving the bridge.
- **Windows** (W1-W9, 2.3.6.1.19) needs Incus's `tpm` device and the virtio drivers added to the install ISO.
