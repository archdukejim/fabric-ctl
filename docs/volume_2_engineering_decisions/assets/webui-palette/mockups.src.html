The clean-up shown here

  • Settings: each part of the form is its own section, heading on top; only fields sit in the field grid; the console-only table spans the card with sized columns, and the note under it reads "Also changed only on the host"; Back and Next share one bar along the bottom.
  • One button height (36 px; 30 px small) for every kind, so the pending bar's Review, Apply and Discard line up.
  • One notice shape for "finish setting up", relaxed security and results; no manual numbers in what people read.
  • Field edges at 3:1, a visible focus ring, a type scale of six sizes, dates written for people.
