## 2.3.9.1 References used in the federation design

- 389 Directory Server: multi-supplier, hub and consumer replication; fractional and certificate-based replication.
- Kea 3.0: the high-availability hook (hot-standby, load-balancing) and TLS for the HA channel.
- BIND 9.20: primaries/secondaries, NOTIFY, TSIG-signed zone transfers, delegation.
- Step-CA: intermediate CAs signed by an external root ("bring your own CA"), path length.
- Keycloak: LDAP user federation; identity brokering (OIDC).
- OpenBao: Raft storage, snapshots (`bao operator raft snapshot save/restore`).
- PostgreSQL: streaming replication and promotion.

### 2.3.9.2.2 Vendor facts checked

- Keycloak's LDAP group mapper: the strategy `LOAD_GROUPS_BY_MEMBER_ATTRIBUTE_RECURSIVELY` (nested groups through
  Active Directory's `LDAP_MATCHING_RULE_IN_CHAIN`), the `READ_ONLY` mode, `groups.dn` and `groups.ldap.filter`:
  Keycloak's source, `GroupMapperConfig.java`
  (github.com/keycloak/keycloak, `federation/ldap/src/main/java/org/keycloak/storage/ldap/mappers/membership/group/`),
  and the Server Administration Guide, "LDAP mappers".
- Keycloak's group mapper searches `groups.dn` with the LDAP provider's own search scope ("configured globally and
  used for user's search"; fabric's provider uses subtree, `searchScope` 2), and `LDAPQuery`'s default is
  `SearchControls.SUBTREE_SCOPE`: Keycloak's source, `GroupLDAPStorageMapper.java` (`createGroupQuery`) and
  `LDAPQuery.java` (github.com/keycloak/keycloak, `federation/ldap/`). So the site mapper may start at the top site's
  OU and pick its groups by name (1.6.8.2.5).
- Keycloak's brute-force lock is cleared with `DELETE /admin/realms/{realm}/attack-detection/brute-force/users/{id}`:
  Keycloak's Admin REST API reference, "Attack Detection".
- Active Directory: the Reset Password extended right `00299570-246d-11d0-a768-00aa006e0529`, the `lockoutTime` reset
  to 0 to unlock, the in-chain matching rule `1.2.840.113556.1.4.1941`, and that an explicit deny ACE is evaluated
  before an inherited allow: Microsoft's documentation ("Control access rights", "Search filter syntax", "Order of
  ACEs in a DACL").

### 2.3.9.2.3 Assumptions no run has confirmed yet

- Samba's Python security descriptor binding removes an explicit ACE with `dacl_del_ace` and writes it back with
  `modify_sd_on_dn` (`acl_entries.remove_aces`); used when a grant is revoked or a person no longer holds a role.
- A site admin with create-child on `user` objects only (no other rights on the OU) can create a person with
  `samba-tool user add` over LDAP, and `samba-tool user setpassword` works with the Reset Password right alone.
- The deny on writing `member` of the site's role groups stops `<site>-admins` from adding themselves even though
  they hold the write on `<site>-users`'s member list (different objects; checked by `roles.py`).

### 2.3.9.2.4 Open: `ipam show` and `ipam export` of a sibling

2.1.9.22 names `ipam find`; `fabricctl ipam show <site>` and `ipam export <site>` still read any site named.
Options: (a) the same rule: refuse a site not shown (a sibling or a parent), the top sees all; (b) leave them as they
are, since they need the site's name and run on the host's own root account. **Recommendation: (a)**, so one rule
covers every view. Built as (b) for now, the narrower change; waits for the owner.

**Settled 2026-10-13: (a)**, built (2.1.9.22's revision; 2.3.9.2.10).

### 2.3.9.2.5 Open: the console role of a site's admins at the sites above

Built: a console bundle given by a site's role group applies at that site only (the second mapper maps only the
site's own `OU=groups`). AD's rights, by contrast, inherit down the OU tree (2.1.6.20). Options: (a) keep it so (chosen,
the narrowest); (b) map the groups of the sites below too, so a granted role at a parent reaches its children's
console. **Recommendation: (a)** until a site with children asks for it.

**Settled 2026-10-13: (b)**, the owner's choice, built (2.1.9.21's second revision; 2.3.9.2.10). In AD as well:
the machine and GPO admins of every site above are given each site's machines and links (manual 1.6.3.18.7).

### 2.3.9.2.6 Open: site admins who are not the site's users

Keycloak admits `<site>-users` and the organisation's admins (`ensure_ldap_federation`). A person in `<site>-admins`
but not in `<site>-users` is not admitted, so gets no console role at all. Options: (a) add `<site>-admins` to the
admitted filter; (b) have convergence nest `<site>-admins` in `<site>-users`; (c) leave it, documented. **Recommendation:
(b)**, which keeps one admitted group. Not built; waits for the owner.

**Settled 2026-10-13: (b)**, built (`narrow_site_admins`; 2.1.9.21's revision; 2.3.9.2.10).

### 2.3.9.2.7 Known limits

- The fabric-agent's account keeps its per-site rights (2.1.9.21), so the root account of a branch host can still
  grant its own site's roles straight in AD; the console and the CLI refuse a self-grant, and AD's deny covers the
  site's admins, not the agent. **Closed 2026-10-13** (2.1.9.21's revision, manual 1.6.3.18.4): the branch's agent
  is denied those groups too; a group it deletes and makes again is refused and replaced from above.
- A host whose DC is writable holds the domain's database: its root can change it below every access entry. The
  layered rule binds a branch through its agent's account; at a site with its own writable DC (the DC promotion work
  of 0.9) it rests on who is trusted with that DC.
- The console's own-site check (`person_guard`) sees direct group memberships; a role held only through a nested group
  is caught by AD (in-chain), not by the console.
- A site behind a read-only DC is narrowed by the converge of a site above it (`_sites_below`); a failure there is
  reported, never fatal.
- AD stays readable domain-wide to authenticated accounts; fabric does not hide that (2.1.9.22).
- Topic numbers 1.9.8.20 and 4.9.1.2 were taken in parallel with the DC promotion work for 0.9; a clash at merge
  takes the next free number (5.7).

### 2.3.9.2.8 Open: a branch's `<site>-admins` group made again by the branch

The branch's agent may still delete `<site>-admins` (delete-child on its OU=groups) and make it again. Convergence then
refuses the site (the group is owned by the agent), so the helpdesk's rights never reach the new group; the branch only
loses its own helpdesk. A grant does not remake it (it is not a grantable role). Today a domain administrator deletes
it and the site's own convergence makes it again where its DC is writable; at a site whose DC is read-only nothing
makes it again. Options: (a) `fabricctl federation roles` at a site above also remakes `<site>-admins` (a `repair`
action); (b) the convergence of a site above remakes any of a site's own groups that are missing (`ensure_groups` from
above: two writable DCs could both make one before replication); (c) leave it to a domain administrator, documented.
**Recommendation: (a)**, explicit and from above. Not built.

**Settled 2026-10-13: (a)**, built (2.1.9.21's third revision): DC operation `remake_site_group` (the helpers it
shares with `grant_site_role` in `role_groups.py`), `fabricctl federation roles remake <site> <role|admins>`, the
agent's `/v1/federation/roles` action `remake`, the Roles card. Tests: `tests/samba/site_roles_test.py` (replaced,
made, left as it is; refused at the site itself, a sibling, a site above, an unknown group: 58 passed),
`tests/federation/roles_test.py` (fabriclib, audit, agent route, CLI, console post: 66 passed), `tests/samba/roles.py`
(a real DC: house1-admins deleted and re-made by its agent, remade by the top, refused from house1: **not run**; needs
the `samba` suite).

### 2.3.9.2.9 Open: people who hold a role, within reach of the branch's service accounts

`protect_privileged_people` denies a site's helpdesk the takeover rights on people who hold a role. The branch's own
agent and Keycloak accounts keep full control of the site's OU=people (1.6.3.6), so root on the branch host can still
reset the password of, or write a certificate mapping on, a person living in that site's OU=people who holds a role —
Central IT living at a branch, or someone granted a role at a site above. Options: (a) deny the branch's agent and
Keycloak account every write on such people (Keycloak then cannot store a password they change at that branch's sign-in
page: they change it at the top); (b) deny the agent only (the Keycloak account's password stays on the host: half a
fix); (c) keep people who hold a role at the top site's OU only (an operator rule). **Recommendation: (a)** with
(c) as the documented practice. Not built: it changes what Keycloak can do at a branch.
