## 2.1.5.1 Name-constrain each site's intermediate to `<site>.<domain>`?

- **Decision:** Proposed: yes for DNS names; people and device certificates use non-DNS names, so check the constraint set against them before deciding.
- **Shapes:** 1.5, 1.9
- **Made:** proposed 2026-10-01 to 2026-10-02, with the federation design; never marked decided.
- **Why:** a site's CA should not be able to issue for another site's names (1.9).
- **Status:** Open: the owner has not answered it.

## 2.1.5.2 Revocation of a removed site's intermediate

- **Decision:** Proposed: a CRL from the root site's CA, published on `certs.<domain>` and checked by every site's FreeRADIUS and nginx (today there is no CRL: fabric relies on unlinking devices).
- **Shapes:** 1.5, 1.9
- **Made:** proposed 2026-10-01 to 2026-10-02, with the federation design; never marked decided.
- **Why:** a removed site's intermediate must stop being trusted everywhere (1.9).
- **Status:** Answered 2026-10-08 by 2.1.5.10: a CRL for everything fabric issues, site intermediates included.

## 2.1.5.3 Let's Encrypt through Cloudflare

- **Decision:** The DC's certificates come from Step-CA like every service; external CAs and Let's Encrypt are a separate milestone.
- **Shapes:** 1.5, 1.6
- **Made:** 2026-10-04, by the owner, answering the Samba design's questions (proposed: its own design after this one, unless pulled forward).
- **Why:** 2.3.6.1.1. Let's Encrypt through Cloudflare's DNS-01 was its own milestone, after 1.0.
- **Status:** In force for the DC (its certificates stay Step-CA's). Revised 2026-10-08 by 2.1.5.9: public certificates through Let's Encrypt and Cloudflare are built in 0.6.3 (since moved to 0.11, 2.1.1.45). External CAs stay after 1.0.

## 2.1.5.4 Service certificates: 47 days, renewed at 30 days old

- **Decision:** `cert_service_days` 47 and `cert_renew_after_days` 30 (settings); a daily timer, `fabric-certs.timer`, renews what needs it and every certificate longer-lived than the setting; picked up by a reload where the service allows it (nginx, Postgres, OpenBao by SIGHUP, Keycloak by itself), a restart otherwise; a failed run or a certificate under 7 days is a warning in status and the web UI and a failure in doctor.
- **Shapes:** 1.5, 1.2, 4.5
- **Made:** 2026-10-08, by the owner, for 0.6.3.
- **Why:** ready for the CA/Browser Forum's 47-day limit of 2029 with a 30-day cycle; renewal exercised all the time (2.3.5.3.3, 2.3.5.3.13).
- **Status:** In force; **built 2026-10-08** for 0.6.3.

## 2.1.5.5 Certificates fabric cannot renew are warned about ahead

- **Decision:** The root and intermediate CA 180 days ahead; the web UI's admin client certificate and issued device and people certificates 30 days ahead (status, doctor, the web UI's overview and PKI page).
- **Shapes:** 1.5, 1.8
- **Made:** 2026-10-08, by the owner, for 0.6.3.
- **Why:** 2.3.5.3.4.
- **Status:** In force; **built 2026-10-08** for 0.6.3.

## 2.1.5.6 The CA's lifetime is asked at install

- **Decision:** The root's lifetime (`cert_root_ca_days`, default 3650) is asked in setup's Advanced, at least 365 days; the intermediate's is the root's less one year; both are fixed once the CA exists (a changed value is refused with the reason).
- **Shapes:** 1.5, 3.3
- **Made:** 2026-10-08, by the owner, for 0.6.3.
- **Why:** 2.3.5.3.5.
- **Status:** In force; **built 2026-10-08** for 0.6.3. Since 2.1.2.16 (2026-10-09) it is one of the few choices left in Advanced: it is permanent.

## 2.1.5.7 Upgrading re-issues long-lived service certificates

- **Decision:** Any service certificate longer-lived than the setting is re-issued; the upgrade to 0.6.3 replaces the 365-day and 5475-day certificates this way.
- **Shapes:** 1.5, 3.3
- **Made:** 2026-10-08, by the owner, for 0.6.3.
- **Why:** 2.3.5.3.3.
- **Status:** In force; **built 2026-10-08** for 0.6.3.

## 2.1.5.8 ACME from fabric's CA for other machines on the LAN

- **Decision:** Step-CA's ACME provisioner issues 47-day certificates for names under fabric's domain only, over http-01 and dns-01; dns-01 through a dynamic zone `acme.<domain>` with a per-machine TSIG key limited to its own name (`fabricctl acme enroll|list|remove`, the PKI page).
- **Shapes:** 1.5, 1.4, 4.5
- **Made:** 2026-10-08, by the owner, for 0.6.3.
- **Why:** 2.3.5.3.14.
- **Status:** In force; built in 0.6.3 (1.5.1.4). Built differently in two details, keeping what was decided: dns-01 uses fabric's per-name TSIG grants in the main zone (only `_acme-challenge.<name>`'s TXT record: the same isolation as a separate `acme.<domain>` zone, with nothing new to run; in the web console, the BIND page's TSIG keys), and the names are limited by the ACME provisioner's own template, since a self-hosted Step-CA enforces policies only for the whole authority.

## 2.1.5.9 Public certificates: a wildcard from Let's Encrypt beside the private CA

- **Decision:** One public wildcard for fabric's domain from Let's Encrypt (any ACME CA) over DNS-01 through Cloudflare (a zone-scoped token in OpenBao), off by default and asked in Advanced; chosen per browser-facing service and per LAN machine (private, its own public certificate, or the shared wildcard, the last shown as a relaxation); machines fetch theirs over HTTPS with their own token. Step-CA stays the private CA: no public CA issues it a subordinate certificate.
- **Shapes:** 1.5, 1.8, 1.12, 4.5
- **Made:** 2026-10-08, by the owner, for 0.6.3.
- **Why:** devices trust the browser-facing pages without fabric's root; a wildcard keeps host names out of the public CT logs (2.3.5.3.15).
- **Status:** In force; **moved to 0.7 by the owner on 2026-10-08** (2.1.1.39): not built in 0.6.3. **Moved again, to the release after 0.7, by 2.1.1.42 on 2026-10-10.** **Placed in 0.11 by 2.1.1.45 on 2026-10-10.**

## 2.1.5.10 Certificate revocation with a CRL, for everything fabric issues

- **Decision:** Step-CA publishes a CRL on `certs.<domain>`, named in every certificate fabric issues; any issued certificate or a site intermediate is revoked from the PKI page or `fabricctl certs revoke` (`pki:revoke`); FreeRADIUS and nginx check it, refreshed by the certificate timer.
- **Shapes:** 1.5, 1.8, 1.11, 1.9
- **Made:** 2026-10-08, by the owner, for 0.6.3.
- **Why:** a revoked device is refused even while linked (2.3.1.11.4); answers 2.1.5.2.
- **Status:** In force; **built 2026-10-08** for 0.6.3.
