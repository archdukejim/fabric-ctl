## 2.3.8.1 The setup wizard: as built, and tested

2026-10-14, branch `dev/0.8.0-wizard` (from `dev/0.8.0-ipam`), building
[1.8.5](../volume_1_description_and_architecture/1.8.5-setup-wizard.md#185-the-setup-wizard-setups-choices-in-the-console-and-in-a-file)
and decision 2.1.8.7 as designed; the details the design left open are in 1.8.5.10.

- **Built:** `fabriclib/settings/` (the wizard's keys, read, export, the file's parsing, each value's check, the plan,
  stale or current, the apply, the wizard's fields both ways, the overview, the finished record, `fabricctl settings`);
  fabric-agent's five routes (`agent/post_settings`, `agent/plan_store`, two reads in `get_route`), `settings:admin`;
  the console's Settings tab (its steps, the plan page, Export, Import, Dismiss), *Finish setting up* on the Overview,
  the first sign-in opening the wizard, the apply job's page; the dev preview's wizard; Guided setup's last step.
- **Split out, for reuse:** `dhcp/common/turn_dhcp_on` (from `set_dhcp_on`), `setup/common/parse_log_target` (from
  `ask_log_target`), `dns_filter/common/parse_upstreams` (from `set_filter_upstreams`); the three callers behave as
  before.
- **Tests written and run** (no container; 2026-10-14, WSL Ubuntu 24.04): `tests/settings/run.py` (79 cases: the round
  trip, the ignored keys by reason, every wizard key's refusals with nothing changed, the plan and the memory plan, a
  stale plan, the apply, the fields, `fabricctl settings` export and import with both answers) and
  `tests/settings/console.py` (43 cases: the routes' permission, the agent's plan, apply job, stale plan made again,
  another person's plan, dismiss; the console's steps, plan page, a refused plan and import, Apply with an old
  sign-in, the export, *Finish setting up*, the first sign-in), all passed; `tests/webui/test_devserver.py` with
  four new wizard cases, 146 passed.
- **Not run** (they need containers or a machine): the `webui` container suite (the pages against a real
  fabric-agent and a mock Keycloak: no wizard case is written there yet), the `kea`, `resolver` and `fluentbit`
  suites after the three split-outs (their callers' behaviour is unchanged; the suites would prove it), and the
  sandbox's end-to-end wizard change and imported file (1.8.5.9).
