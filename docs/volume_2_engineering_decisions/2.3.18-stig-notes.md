## 2.3.18.1 The research of 2026-10-22: DISA's library and where the versions came from

### 2.3.18.1.1 Sources, and how far each was trusted

- **DISA's library**, `https://public.cyber.mil/stigs/downloads/` (redirects to `https://www.cyber.mil/stigs/downloads/`):
  a script-rendered page; fetched without a browser it returns only an empty shell, so no version was read there.
- **cyber.trackr.live** (`https://cyber.trackr.live/stig`), a mirror built from DISA's quarterly archive: its index gave
  every version below, and its per-guide pages the rule lists and DISA's digests of changes. Its long pages were read
  only in part (a fetch returns about 100,000 characters), so the rule ranges after the points named in the checklist's
  header are approximate.
- **NIST's National Checklist Program** (`https://ncp.nist.gov/checklist/`), which lists DISA's downloads with their
  SHA-256 hashes: the GPO packages' file names and the Windows Server 2025 STIG's V1R3.
- **stigviewer.com**, vendors' notes (BigFix, Tenable, HCL AppScan), Samba's wiki and source tree, Ubuntu's
  documentation: as said with each item.
- Where two sources disagree it is said; every version is confirmed at DISA's download before its rows are answered in
  the per-rule pass (2.1.18.17).

### 2.3.18.1.2 The versions

The tables of 1.18.1.3 hold them. Particulars:

- The ASD STIG is at **V6R5** (2026-09-30, 286 rules); HCL's AppScan documents report formats for V5R3 and V6R3, so V6
  has been out for some time. DISA's 2026 texts say "DoW" (Department of War) where earlier ones said DoD.
- The **API SRG** is new: V1R0.1 on 2025-05-16, **V1R1** on 2025-09-11 (65 rules; 54 changes from V1R0.1).
- The **Application Server SRG** is at **V4R6** (2026-09-30); V4R6 raised V-204833 (log transfer for interconnected
  systems) to CAT I. stigviewer's page still showed V4R4 (2025-09-10).
- The **DNS SRG** is at **V4R3** (2026-09-30), whose changes include V-279958 (IETF conformance) and V-279965 (no
  recursion on authoritative servers).
- The **BIND 9.x STIG** is at **V3R4** (2026-09-30, 73 rules); its digest against V3R3 lists V-272371, V-272392,
  V-272394, V-272414 (keys at most a year) and V-272436 (FIPS signatures and hashes). Trackr dates the digest
  2026-07-01, before the release: one of the two dates is wrong.
- **Windows Server 2025:** the trackr index lists V1R4 (2026-09-30); NIST lists V1R3, stigviewer V1R1 (2026-02-20) and
  V1R2 (2026-05-28), BigFix V1R2's SCAP benchmark (2026-08-06). V1R4 is taken as current, to confirm.
- **Windows 11:** V2R11 (2026-09-30); BigFix lists the V2R10 benchmark on 2026-09-01.
- **Ubuntu 24.04:** V1R7 (2026-09-30, 195 rules); Ubuntu's documentation still cites V1R4 (2025-02-25).
- **GPO package:** quarterly, `U_STIG_GPO_Package_<Month>_<Year>.zip` at
  `https://dl.dod.cyber.mil/wp-content/uploads/stigs/zip/`; NIST lists January, April and July 2026 (October 2025's hash
  `0DECDFE12557BB876EE0C8F07DE7086AEDD7866FB2833B5E78DC18197F3DC929`). NIST shows two different hashes for the July
  2026 file, so a hash is compared at DISA's own page (2.1.18.11).

### 2.3.18.1.3 Searched for and not found

- **A certificate authority guide:** no current DISA STIG or SRG for a CA product or PKI service is in the 2026 library;
  the general guides cover step-ca and OpenBao (1.18.1.3.2), as the owner chose.
- **Ubuntu 26.04 LTS:** no DISA STIG (Ubuntu 26.04.1 is out; DISA has published each Ubuntu LTS's STIG some months
  after it).
- **No STIG for** Keycloak, Samba, OpenBao or HashiCorp Vault, step-ca, AdGuard Home, Kea or any DHCP server, FreeRADIUS
  or any RADIUS server, chrony, Fluent Bit, Debian or Proxmox VE. Their closest guides are 1.18.1.3.3's.
- **Not confirmed:** the Windows Defender Firewall STIG's current version (not reached in the index); the exact
  contents of the 2026 GPO packages (not downloaded: large, and not to be kept in the repository); the newest packages'
  hashes from DISA itself.

## 2.3.18.3 The members' OS STIGs: what is DoD's, and what meets fabric's design

- **Ubuntu 24.04 V1R7** (the rules seen): DoD banners for SSH and the graphical greeter (V-270691 to V-270693; V-270694
  removed in V1R7); DoW PKI CAs (V-270745); PIV and smart cards (V-270672, V-270673, V-270721 to V-270723); audit
  offload to another system (V-270658); AIDE telling the ISSO (V-270652); 35-day inactivity (V-270683); 72-hour
  temporary accounts (V-270682); 180-day passwords (V-270731); sssd installed and used (V-270662, V-270663); SSH
  ciphers, MACs and key exchange (V-270667 to V-270671); auditd and its rules (V-270656, V-270657, V-270676, V-270684,
  V-270685, V-270831, V-270832, V-274870, V-288818); AIDE (V-270649 to V-270652); ufw (V-270654, V-270655); AppArmor
  (V-270659, V-270660); CAT I: no telnet (V-270647), single-user mode authenticated (V-270675), no NFS server
  (V-279938). sssd being required fits fabric's join (1.6.7) as it is.
- **Windows 11 and Server 2025** (from DISA's earlier releases, to confirm in the package): the DoD legal notice
  (`LegalNoticeCaption`, `LegalNoticeText`); DoD roots and the Interoperability and CCEB cross-certificates (the `PK`
  rules); user rights: "Allow log on locally" to Administrators and Users, Remote Desktop to Administrators only on
  servers, and denies on members of Enterprise and Domain Admins (network, batch, service, local, Remote Desktop) and on
  "Local account" (network and Remote Desktop). The allow rights collide with the grants' GPOs, which own them
  (1.18.3.3.2 a); the top-tier denies match 2.1.6.68; the local-account denies meet `local-admin` (2.1.18.12). Account
  policies in a member-linked GPO reach local accounts only.
- **Precedence:** fabric links its GPOs at `OU=machines` and keeps `fabric: access none` first in `gPLink`, every set's
  access GPO after it; sssd and Windows take a right whole from the GPO later in that string (checked on sssd,
  2.3.6.19.1). A baseline placed between the two cannot override a set's rights, and fabric strips them from it anyway.

## 2.3.18.4 Group Policy on members: the mechanics

### 2.3.18.4.1 DISA's GPO package

A zip of GPMC backups by product (each GPO a folder with `Backup.xml`, `bkupInfo.xml`, `gpreport.xml` and its
`DomainSysvol` files: `Registry.pol`, `GptTmpl.inf`, `audit.csv`), the ADMX templates, GPO reports, WMI filter exports
and checklist files, with a ReadMe (Microsoft Q&A's description of the Windows 10 package; Quest's import notes). Samba
serves any GPO's SYSVOL files to Windows, so a backup's files copied into a new GPO, with its extension names, apply as
they would from Windows. `samba-tool gpo restore` reads only `samba-tool gpo backup`'s own format, not a GPMC backup,
so fabric writes the GPO itself (1.18.3.3.3), as its ADMX editor does.

### 2.3.18.4.2 samba-gpupdate on Linux members

- **Extensions** in Samba's tree (`python/samba/gp/`): `gp_sec_ext` (password and Kerberos policy: Samba DCs only),
  `gp_scripts_ext` (cron scripts), `gp_sudoers_ext`, `gp_smb_conf_ext`, `gp_msgs_ext` (motd and issue),
  `gp_cert_auto_enroll_ext` (Samba 4.16+, certmonger and cepces, needs AD CS), `gp_chromium_ext`, `gp_firefox_ext`,
  `gp_gnome_settings_ext`, `gp_firewalld_ext`, `gp_drive_maps_ext`, `gp_centrify_sudoers_ext`,
  `gp_centrify_crontab_ext`, and the Vintela-compatible `vgp_access_ext`, `vgp_files_ext`, `vgp_issue_ext`,
  `vgp_motd_ext`, `vgp_openssh_ext`, `vgp_startup_scripts_ext`, `vgp_sudoers_ext`, `vgp_symlink_ext`. Server side:
  `samba-tool gpo manage` (Samba 4.15+) for scripts, files, symlinks, sudoers, motd, issue, access and openssh;
  `samba-tool gpo admxload` for Samba's templates.
- **Behaviour** (Samba's wiki, "Group Policy"): applied every 90 to 120 seconds; `--force`, `--rsop`, `--unapply`; not
  tattooed (taken off when a GPO stops applying). Winbind members turn it on with `apply group policies = yes`; sssd
  members use `oddjob-gpupdate`, which Debian does not package (Debian bug #1068360's thread, 2024). On an sssd member
  the machine's password must also be in Samba's `secrets.tdb`: `adcli join --add-samba-data` or sssd's
  `ad_update_samba_machine_account_password`.
- **What it cannot do:** no security template on members, so no account, audit or user-rights policy; nothing of auditd,
  PAM quality, kernel parameters, packages, AIDE or AppArmor but by script. Hence 2.1.18.13's recommendation.

### 2.3.18.4.3 Ubuntu's USG and ComplianceAsCode

- **USG** (Ubuntu Pro): `usg fix disa_stig`, `usg generate-fix disa_stig --output fix.sh` for many machines, audit and
  tailoring; FIPS through `pro enable fips-updates`; Ubuntu's own advice is to apply it to fresh installs only, since the
  profile changes essential configuration. It needs an Ubuntu Pro subscription per machine.
- **ComplianceAsCode** (the SCAP Security Guide, BSD-3-Clause) holds the STIG profiles USG and RHEL's
  `scap-security-guide` are built from; OpenSCAP (`oscap xccdf eval --profile ... --remediate --tailoring-file ...`)
  applies and scans with it. Which Ubuntu releases its current release covers is to be confirmed when 2.1.18.13 is
  decided.
