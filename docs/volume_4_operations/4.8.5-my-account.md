## 4.8.5.1 Signing in with no fabric role

Design: [1.8.6.6](../volume_1_description_and_architecture/1.8.6-console-redo.md#1866-my-account). Every person of the
domain may sign in to the console. Someone with no fabric role (no fabric group) sees only **My account**; each fabric
group adds its own entries. Signing in still needs what the console asks of everyone (the client certificate when it
is required, the second factor); a disabled account cannot sign in.

## 4.8.5.2 Your details, certificates and devices

**My account** shows your details as the domain has them (name, e-mail, phone, title, department, manager and the
rest), the groups you are in, the certificates fabric issued to you (with whether each is valid, expires soon, has
expired or was revoked) and the 802.1X devices you own. You cannot change your details here: ask the helpdesk.

## 4.8.5.3 Changing your password

1. Under **Change my password**, type your current password and the new one twice.
2. **Change my password** changes it in the domain at once (no Apply). The domain's password policy applies; a
   refusal says why and changes nothing.
3. Your other signed-in sessions are signed out; the one you used stays.

Forgotten your password? Fabric sends no mail: ask the helpdesk to reset your sign-in. Your second factors
(authenticator app, passkeys) are on the sign-in service's own page: **Manage my sign-in factors**.

## 4.8.5.4 Groups you manage

**Groups I manage** lists each group you are a manager of, its members, and what you may do: add members, remove
them, or both (sometimes only people of one site). Each change waits for **Apply** in the top bar, as on every page
([4.8.4](4.8.4-apply.md#4841-changes-wait-for-apply)); the Review shows only your own applies.
