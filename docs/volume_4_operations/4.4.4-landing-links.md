## 4.4.4.1 Landing Page Links

The landing page provides a grid of quick links for the infrastructure. You can add, modify, or delete these links dynamically using the interactive menu. 

All custom links are saved in `link-vars.yaml` and are deployed alongside the normal variables. They natively evaluate Jinja variables such as `{{ domain }}` to ensure links stay accurate even if the base domain changes.

```yaml
links:
  - name: NAS
    link: "nas.{{ domain }}"
  - name: Keycloak (Admin)
    link: "sso.{{ domain }}/admin"
```

## 4.4.4.2 Linking your records from the landing page (0.7.1)

Design: [1.4.2.4](../volume_1_description_and_architecture/1.4.2-landing-links.md#1424-the-landing-pages-own-section-of-the-dns-tab-071).
The landing page is fabric's start page, `info.<domain>`. Besides fabric's own links it can link to your services,
from the records of the site's zone. Needs `dns:write`.

1. BIND9 tab → **Landing page**. The list shows each link: its label, the address it opens and its record.
2. *Add a link*: pick the **record** (A, AAAA or CNAME), and give a **label** (the text shown; default: the name), a
   **port** (e.g. `8006` for Proxmox VE) and a **path** (starting with `/`) when the service needs them. **Link it**.
   Linking a record again replaces its link.
3. *Remove* stops linking a record (the record stays).
4. **Apply changes** publishes the landing page.

The links are kept on the records (`link:` in the settings file), as since 0.6.1.
