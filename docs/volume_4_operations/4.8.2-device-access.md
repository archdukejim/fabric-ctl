## 4.8.2.1 Device access (RBAC)

People live in the domain (Samba AD, [1.6.3](../volume_1_description_and_architecture/1.6.3-samba-directory.md)) and sign in
through **Keycloak**. The domain also holds the *devices* and what they may do. Device permissions are a separate
set from the people's fabric permissions in [Who may do what](../volume_1_description_and_architecture/1.8.2-webui-security.md#18211-who-may-do-what):
they say what a device may do on the network, not what a person may do in
this web UI.

- **Device** (`CN=<name>,OU=devices,<ad_base_dn>` — the domain's `OU=devices`, 0.8.0; until then this
  site's OU): type, MAC addresses (each MAC belongs to one device only), owner
  (a user), description, enabled flag, the roles it is in (`fabricRoleName`)
  and the SHA-256 fingerprints of certificates issued to it.
- **Role** (an AD group in the domain's `OU=device-roles`, any site may use it):
  permissions, an optional VLAN and a priority. Its members are the devices
  that name it.
- **Default roles** (created once by setup, then yours to edit or delete):
  `workstations`, `phones-tablets`, `servers` (`network:eap-tls`,
  workstations and servers also `pki:acme`), `printers`, `iot`
  (`network:mab`) and `network-gear` (`pki:scep`); no VLANs.
- **Effective access** is the union of a device's roles' permissions. The
  VLAN comes from the role with the lowest priority number. A disabled
  device gets nothing.

| Device permission | Grants | Enforced by (once installed) |
|---|---|---|
| `network:eap-tls` | Join the network with its certificate (802.1X EAP-TLS) | FreeRADIUS |
| `network:mab` | Join the network by MAC address (printers, IoT) | FreeRADIUS |
| `dns:dhcp-register` | Register its DHCP hostname in the DHCP zone | Kea DHCP-DDNS |
| `pki:acme` | Obtain and renew certificates by ACME | Step-CA |
| `pki:scep` | Enrol certificates by SCEP (MDM, network gear) | Step-CA |

FreeRADIUS enforces `network:eap-tls` and `network:mab` (and the VLAN)
when 802.1X is on; `dns:dhcp-register`, `pki:acme` and `pki:scep` are not
enforced yet. The schema is fabric's own, added to AD's:
`fabricDevice`, `fabricRole`, OIDs under Microsoft's arc for unregistered
extensions ([1.6.3.5](../volume_1_description_and_architecture/1.6.3-samba-directory.md#1635-fabrics-schema-in-ad)).

The web UI changes the directory through fabric-agent bound as
**`fabric-agent-dc-NNNN`**, this DC's service account (`ad_agent_password`;
[1.6.13.10](../volume_1_description_and_architecture/1.6.13-access-by-grants.md#161310-what-d1-built-080)). Every change is audited
(`DEVICE_*`, `ROLE_*`).

## 4.8.2.2 In the console: Devices and Device roles

Design: [1.8.6.11.13](../volume_1_description_and_architecture/1.8.6-console-redo.md#18611-as-built).

- **802.1X → Devices** (needs `devices:read`): each device with its state, type, MACs, owner, roles and what its roles
  add up to. Select a device for its page.
- **Add device** (needs `devices:enroll`): its name, type, owner (chosen from People when you may read them), MACs,
  description, roles and whether it is enabled; **Add device**, then **Apply** in the top bar (4.8.4).
- **A device's page:** what it may do and from which role its VLAN comes; its certificates, with **Generate key +
  certificate** and **Sign its CSR** in the actions bar (with `pki:issue` / `pki:sign`) and **Unlink certificate**
  (with `pki:link-device`); **Save the device** and **Remove device** (with `devices:admin`). Each waits for
  **Apply**.
- **802.1X → Device roles:** each role with its priority, VLAN, grants and how many devices it holds. **Add device
  role** and a role's **Save the role** and **Remove device role** need `roles:admin` and wait for **Apply** on Device
  roles. A role that still holds devices is refused at Apply: take its devices out first, on each device's page.
