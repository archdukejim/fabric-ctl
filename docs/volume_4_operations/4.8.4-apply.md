## 4.8.4.1 Changes wait for Apply

Design: [1.8.6.5](../volume_1_description_and_architecture/1.8.6-console-redo.md#1865-apply-per-page). From 0.8.0 the
Directory's pages (People, Groups, Machines, Policies) do not change anything when you press a form's button: the
change waits on its page until you apply it. Other pages join as they are rebuilt.

1. **Make the changes.** Each says "Waiting for Apply: …" and the top bar shows "N changes pending" with **Review**,
   **Apply** and **Discard**; the navigation shows the number beside the page. Your pending changes are yours alone:
   nobody else sees them, and another page's Apply never applies them.
2. **Review** lists each change in the order it will run, what it was and what it becomes, with **Drop** to remove
   one.
3. **Apply** runs them in order as one job. The Review shows each change's progress (queued, running, done, failed
   with why) and refreshes itself until the job ends. The first change that fails stops the job; it and the changes
   after it stay pending, to drop or apply again.
4. A change to something another person changed since you made it is refused, and shown again against how it is
   now ("Changed by someone else since"): check it, then **Apply** again.
5. A one-time password (a new person's, a reset sign-in, a new machine's join password) is shown once on the
   Review after Apply, under "Shown only now". It is not kept: write it down or hand it over then.
6. **Discard** drops every pending change of the page.

Pending changes live in fabric-agent's memory: a restart of the host or of `fabric-agent` drops them (make them
again). Everyone who may see a page sees its queue (who applied what, and how it went), not its pending changes.
Changing your own password is never pending: it is done at once.
