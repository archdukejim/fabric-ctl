The pencil changes a record in its row, the bin deletes it, the last row adds one. Every change on DNS → Zones (records, zones, links, a TSIG key's names, removing a TSIG key) waits for Apply in the top bar, which reloads only BIND (and writes the landing page); a changed record stays as it was until its new form is accepted.

The value is the address for A and AAAA, the host it points to for CNAME, MX and SRV, the text for TXT; MX and SRV take a priority, SRV a weight and a port too. Names marked provided by fabric are fabric's own: every apply adds them back from their setting, so they are kept by fabric.
