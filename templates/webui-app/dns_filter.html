Your rules everyone, and every group

Each name counts with every name below it. An allowed name passes every list; a blocked name is blocked whatever the lists say. fabric's own names are never filtered. A client group's own rules are on its page (Groups). A change takes a second or two.

Client groups

A group is a set of addresses and subnets with its own safe search and rules, added to everyone's; the lists are the site's, for every client: a group can allow names past them, not switch one off. A client in no group is answered as everyone. When a client is in a group's subnet and another group names its address, the address wins.
