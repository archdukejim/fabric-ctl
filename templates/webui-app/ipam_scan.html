Scan

Pings every address of one subnet of this site's plan (DHCP on or off) and reads which devices answered ARP: what answers but nobody recorded is shown, and you may record it. A scan never blocks anything. A routed VLAN answers only what its gateway lets through, and shows no MAC. One scan at a time; the same subnet once a minute; at most a /22.
